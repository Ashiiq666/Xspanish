About X Spanish

Who We Are

Menswear The Way It Should Feel

X Spanish opened its doors in 2009 in the heart of Feroke with a single goal — to make great menswear feel personal again. No piles of clothes to dig through. No salespeople reading from a script. Just a team that genuinely loves clothes and wants to find the right fit for you.

Today we run two stores — Feroke and Ramanattukara — and stock a carefully chosen line-up of brands. From everyday essentials to wedding-day showstoppers, we've built the rack you'll actually want to shop from.
